Accessibility Audit — Settings Card (HomeScreen)

Scope
- Component: Settings modal card in apps/mobile/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx
- Background: solid colors.bgCard (#0f2e0f) with low-opacity texture overlay image
- Text: primary labels and title use #ffffff

WCAG 2.1 AA Contrast (Normal Text)
- Required minimum: 4.5:1
- Computed contrast ratio for #ffffff on #0f2e0f: 14.81:1

Texture Overlay
- Image: apps/mobile/assets/e27c2e8e377e60057010a8431706b96b0152436f.png
- Applied opacity: 0.06
- Impact: negligible on contrast due to very low opacity; base contrast remains far above 4.5:1.

Result
- Pass: ≥ 4.5:1 contrast maintained for primary text against the settings card background.
